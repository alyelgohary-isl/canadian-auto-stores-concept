Old-fashioned family service. A very modern inventory.

Canadian Auto Stores has been an independent, family-owned parts retailer in Windsor since 1938. The counter staff are the reason people come back: they know the catalogue, they know the cars, and you'll never wait long for service.

Garages order by the case; do-it-yourselfers come in with the old part in a bag. Both get the same answer: we have it, or we'll get it.
